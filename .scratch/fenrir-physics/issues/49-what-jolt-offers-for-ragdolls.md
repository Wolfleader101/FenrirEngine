# What does Jolt offer for ragdolls?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What do JoltPhysics v5.6.0's ragdoll and skeleton helpers offer to, and require of, FenrirPhysics, in the areas where those facts constrain the ragdoll spec? Answer from Jolt's own source, docs and samples (the submodule at `packages/FenrirPhysics/libs/JoltPhysics`, `Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/`, `Jolt/Skeleton/`, `Jolt/Physics/Collision/GroupFilterTable.h`, `Samples/Tests/Rig/` and `Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.*`), pinned to v5.6.0. FenrirPhysics already builds joints one Jolt constraint at a time from `Joint` components (ADR-0013), with a private `GroupFilter` over a pair table for `collideConnected` (joints ticket "How do jointed bodies stop colliding?"), so the question is which of Jolt's ragdoll helpers add anything behind that seam, and what they would need from it:

- **`RagdollSettings` and `Ragdoll`:** what a part is (a `BodyCreationSettings` plus the constraint to its parent, one per skeleton joint, plus additional constraints); what `CreateRagdoll`, `AddToPhysicsSystem` and `RemoveFromPhysicsSystem` do that adding bodies and constraints one at a time does not; what `Stabilize` changes (and whether it is for constraints created separately); what `CalculateConstraintPriorities` does to the solve order and whether FenrirPhysics's private joint-entity-order priority already gives the same; whether a `Ragdoll` can be made from bodies and constraints that already exist, or only from settings.
- **Collision inside a ragdoll:** what `DisableParentChildCollisions` sets up (`GroupFilterTable`, one `GroupID` per ragdoll, `SubGroupID` per part, parent–child pairs disabled, and the pose-based overlap detection with its separation distance); what `GroupFilterTable` costs and how it relates to the `GroupFilter` FenrirPhysics already installs on jointed bodies (one `CollisionGroup` per body: can a body be in a joint pair table *and* a ragdoll group?); how `CollisionGroup`s interact with object layers and queries.
- **Posing:** `SetPose` (both overloads, root offset and joint matrices), `GetPose`, `ResetWarmStart`; what space the joint matrices are in and whether they are the bodies' centre-of-mass transforms or the skeleton joints' (and how `RagdollLoader` relates body shapes to joints).
- **Driving:** `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` (velocities to reach a pose in `dt`: what it sets, and whether it needs kinematic bodies); `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` (both overloads: which constraint kinds it drives, how it turns a pose into `SwingTwistConstraint` and `HingeConstraint` motor targets, position and velocity, and what it does with other kinds); where motor strength lives (constraint motor settings, `MaxTorque`, spring); whether any of this is reproducible from FenrirPhysics's existing `JointMotor`/`SwingTwistMotor` without the `Ragdoll` class.
- **Whole-ragdoll operations:** `SetLinearAndAngularVelocity`, `AddImpulse`, `Activate`, `IsActive`, `GetWorldSpaceBounds`, `GetRootTransform`, `SetGroupID`; which are convenience loops over bodies.
- **Skeleton helpers:** `Skeleton` (named joints with parents; what `AreJointsCorrectlyOrdered` requires), `SkeletonPose` (joint states, matrices, root offset, `CalculateJointMatrices`, `CalculateJointStates`), `SkeletalAnimation`, and `SkeletonMapper` (mapping a high-detail animation skeleton to a low-detail ragdoll skeleton by name, chains for unmapped joints, locked translations, `Map` and `MapReverse`): what each does, and whether the mapping of an animation skeleton onto fewer ragdoll bodies is something FenrirPhysics would want behind the seam or something the glue would do with FenrirSkeleton's `SkeletonData`.
- **Kinematic and dynamic switching:** what the samples do to switch a ragdoll between following an animation and simulating (`KinematicRigTest`, `PoweredRigTest`, `SoftKeyframedRigTest`), motion types used, and what Jolt requires when a body's motion type changes while constrained.
- **What the samples do:** `CreateRigTest`'s human ragdoll (which bodies, shapes, sizes and masses; which constraint kind for which joint; limit values), `RagdollLoader`'s two constraint variants (swing-twist and ragdoll/six-DOF), `PoweredRigTest`'s motor settings (frequency, damping, max torque), `RigPileTest`'s counts and costs, `LoadRigTest`'s format; as starting points for FenrirPhysics's defaults.
- **Threading, timing and determinism:** which `Ragdoll` calls lock bodies, which may run while the step runs, and what order-dependence they add.
- **Debug drawing:** anything ragdoll-specific `DebugRenderer` draws, since FenrirPhysics draws its own debug lines.

Put the findings on a `research/jolt-ragdolls` branch, in `.scratch/fenrir-physics/research/jolt-ragdolls.md`, and link them here.
