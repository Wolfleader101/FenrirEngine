# What does Jolt offer for ragdolls?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What do JoltPhysics v5.6.0's ragdoll and skeleton helpers offer to, and require of, FenrirPhysics, in the areas where those facts constrain the ragdoll spec? Answer from Jolt's own source, docs and samples (the submodule at `packages/FenrirPhysics/libs/JoltPhysics`, `Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/`, `Jolt/Skeleton/`, `Jolt/Physics/Collision/GroupFilterTable.h`, `Samples/Tests/Rig/` and `Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.*`), pinned to v5.6.0. FenrirPhysics already builds joints one Jolt constraint at a time from `Joint` components (ADR-0013), with a private `GroupFilter` over a pair table for `collideConnected` (joints ticket "How do jointed bodies stop colliding?"), so the question is which of Jolt's ragdoll helpers add anything behind that seam, and what they would need from it:

- **`RagdollSettings` and `Ragdoll`:** what a part is (a `BodyCreationSettings` plus the constraint to its parent, one per skeleton joint, plus additional constraints); what `CreateRagdoll`, `AddToPhysicsSystem` and `RemoveFromPhysicsSystem` do that adding bodies and constraints one at a time does not; what `Stabilize` changes (and whether it is for constraints created separately); what `CalculateConstraintPriorities` does to the solve order and whether FenrirPhysics's private joint-entity-order priority already gives the same; whether a `Ragdoll` can be made from bodies and constraints that already exist, or only from settings.
- **Collision inside a ragdoll:** what `DisableParentChildCollisions` sets up (`GroupFilterTable`, one `GroupID` per ragdoll, `SubGroupID` per part, parent–child pairs disabled, and the pose-based overlap detection with its separation distance); what `GroupFilterTable` costs and how it relates to the `GroupFilter` FenrirPhysics already installs on jointed bodies (one `CollisionGroup` per body: can a body be in a joint pair table *and* a ragdoll group?); how `CollisionGroup`s interact with object layers and queries.
- **Posing:** `SetPose` (both overloads, root offset and joint matrices), `GetPose`, `ResetWarmStart`; what space the joint matrices are in and whether they are the bodies' centre-of-mass transforms or the skeleton joints' (and how `RagdollLoader` relates body shapes to joints).
- **Driving:** `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` (velocities to reach a pose in `dt`: what it sets, and whether it needs kinematic bodies); `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` (both overloads: which constraint kinds it drives, how it turns a pose into `SwingTwistConstraint` and `HingeConstraint` motor targets, position and velocity, and what it does with other kinds); where motor strength lives (constraint motor settings, `MaxTorque`, spring); whether any of this is reproducible from FenrirPhysics's existing `JointMotor`/`SwingTwistMotor` without the `Ragdoll` class.
- **Whole-ragdoll operations:** `SetLinearAndAngularVelocity`, `AddImpulse`, `Activate`, `IsActive`, `GetWorldSpaceBounds`, `GetRootTransform`, `SetGroupID`; which are convenience loops over bodies.
- **Skeleton helpers:** `Skeleton` (named joints with parents; what `AreJointsCorrectlyOrdered` requires), `SkeletonPose` (joint states, matrices, root offset, `CalculateJointMatrices`, `CalculateJointStates`), `SkeletalAnimation`, and `SkeletonMapper` (mapping a high-detail animation skeleton to a low-detail ragdoll skeleton by name, chains for unmapped joints, locked translations, `Map` and `MapReverse`): what each does, and whether the mapping of an animation skeleton onto fewer ragdoll bodies is something FenrirPhysics would want behind the seam or something the glue would do with FenrirSkeleton's `SkeletonData`.
- **Kinematic and dynamic switching:** what the samples do to switch a ragdoll between following an animation and simulating (`KinematicRigTest`, `PoweredRigTest`, `SoftKeyframedRigTest`), motion types used, and what Jolt requires when a body's motion type changes while constrained.
- **What the samples do:** `CreateRigTest`'s human ragdoll (which bodies, shapes, sizes and masses; which constraint kind for which joint; limit values), `RagdollLoader`'s two constraint variants (swing-twist and ragdoll/six-DOF), `PoweredRigTest`'s motor settings (frequency, damping, max torque), `RigPileTest`'s counts and costs, `LoadRigTest`'s format; as starting points for FenrirPhysics's defaults.
- **Threading, timing and determinism:** which `Ragdoll` calls lock bodies, which may run while the step runs, and what order-dependence they add.
- **Debug drawing:** anything ragdoll-specific `DebugRenderer` draws, since FenrirPhysics draws its own debug lines.

Put the findings on a `research/jolt-ragdolls` branch, in `.scratch/fenrir-physics/research/jolt-ragdolls.md`, and link them here.

## Answer

Findings: branch `research/jolt-ragdolls`, `.scratch/fenrir-physics/research/jolt-ragdolls.md` (commit e4fc031f), read against Jolt v5.6.0 (`e77f175`), every claim cited to file and line. Two corrections to this ticket's premises: v5.6.0's `RagdollLoader` has no six-DOF variant (its `TypeRagdoll` keeps the asset's swing-twist; the others are fixed, point, hinge, slider, cone), and `PoweredRigTest`'s motor settings live in `Assets/Human.tof`, not the test.

- **`RagdollSettings` and `Ragdoll`:** a part is a `BodyCreationSettings` plus the constraint settings to its parent, one per skeleton bone, plus additional constraints by body-index pair; `CreateRagdoll` creates bodies (parent body 1, child body 2) and constraints without adding them; `AddToPhysicsSystem` is a batch broad-phase insert plus `AddConstraints`; `~Ragdoll` destroys bodies without removing them. Every runtime method is a loop over `BodyInterface` or a per-constraint motor write, and a `Ragdoll` can only be made from settings (private members, `RagdollSettings` its sole friend). `Stabilize` touches no constraint: it clamps child:parent mass ratios to [0.8, 1.2], raises each part's inertia to at least min(2× own, children's sum) and converts every part to `MassAndInertiaProvided`. `CalculateConstraintPriorities` gives each part base + its longest distance to a leaf so root joints solve last, siblings tying on list index; `mConstraintPriority` is one number, so FenrirPhysics's entity-order priority (its determinism) and root-last cannot both hold.
- **Collision inside a ragdoll:** `DisableParentChildCollisions` builds one `GroupFilterTable` (N(N−1)/2 bits), disables each bone–parent pair, optionally collides every remaining pair in a given pose with a separation distance and disables the hits, and sets `SubGroupID` = bone index; `GroupID` is per instance and must be unique in the system. Rules: invalid group collides, different groups collide, same group with different filter objects does not, same sub-group does not, else the bit. A body holds one `CollisionGroup`, and Jolt asks the first non-null filter of a pair, so a body cannot be in FenrirPhysics's pair table and a `GroupFilterTable` at once, and mixing filter objects in one world is order-dependent: everything a table says is a set of body pairs. The group filter runs after broad-phase and object-layer filters, in simulation and CCD only; queries use `BodyFilter` and always hit ragdoll parts.
- **Posing:** `SetPose` sets each body's origin (not its centre of mass) to root offset + joint translation with the joint's rotation, `DontActivate`, no velocity: Jolt's pose contract is "bone matrix = body origin transform", with the shape's offset inside the body (the samples wrap each capsule in a `StaticCompoundShape`). `GetPose` reads body 0 as the root offset. `ResetWarmStart` deactivates every constraint's impulses, for after a large `SetPose`, not every frame.
- **Driving:** `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` is `MoveKinematic` per body (velocity = delta/dt, wakes if moving) and works on dynamic bodies too (`SoftKeyframedRigTest` cancels gravity with `AddLinearVelocity(g·dt)`). `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` reads each bone's local rotation, sets swing-twist motors to `Position` with a body-space target clamped into the limits, or a hinge's motor to its angle projection, asserts on any other kind and never drives additional constraints; the two-pose overload uses `PositionAndVelocity` with the angular velocity from the quaternion delta. Strength is per constraint in `MotorSettings` (spring 2 Hz, damping 1 by default; torque limits applied as dt·limit impulses; friction torque when off). FenrirPhysics's `SwingTwistMotor` and `JointMotor` make the same calls; missing only `PositionAndVelocity` and the body-to-joint-space conversion.
- **Whole-ragdoll operations:** plain loops: `Activate`, velocity setters and `AddImpulse` through `BodyInterface`; `IsActive`, `GetPose` and `GetWorldSpaceBounds` lock all bodies for read; `SetGroupID` for write; `GetRootTransform` is body 0.
- **Skeleton helpers:** `Skeleton` is named bones with parent name and index, linear lookup, no uniqueness, parents before children asserted; `SkeletonPose` is local states plus model matrices and a root offset; `SkeletalAnimation` is a sample clip format FenrirAnimation supersedes. `SkeletonMapper` is pure matrix code: name-matched mappings with `neutral1⁻¹ × neutral2`, longest chains of unmapped bones re-aimed, unmapped bones from their locals, optional locked translations to hide joint stretch; its forward direction is ADR-0038's override write, and chain re-aiming and translation locking are glue policies over `SkeletonData`.
- **Kinematic and dynamic switching:** no sample switches at run time (`KinematicRigTest` kinematic with velocities, `PoweredRigTest` dynamic with motors, `SoftKeyframedRigTest` dynamic with velocities and a gravity cancel). A switch needs `MotionProperties` (`mAllowDynamicOrKinematic` or non-static creation); to-Kinematic resets force and torque; a two-body constraint is active only with one dynamic body, so a fully kinematic ragdoll's joints are skipped, not solved.
- **What the samples do:** `sCreate` is a 12-part capsule human, all swing-twist, frames equal in world space: spine 5° twist and 10° cones, head 90/45/45, shoulders 45 twist, 90 normal, 45 plane, elbows and knees a zero normal cone (45° twist; knee plane 60°), hips 45/45/45; masses from density then `Stabilize`, priorities, parent–child disable without a pose. `Human.tof` (needs `JPH_OBJECT_STREAM`, which FenrirPhysics builds off) is 23 compound-shaped parts, 22 swing-twist, friction 1, restitution 0.15, damping 0.05, motors 20 Hz, damping 2, ±1000 N m, friction torque 5 N m. `RigPileTest` is 160 ragdolls with unique group ids, posed and motor-held before adding.
- **Threading, timing and determinism:** every body-touching call takes a lock flag; `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` and `ResetWarmStart` write constraints, allowed only between steps; `Update` holds all locks, so every `Ragdoll` call is a between-step call. Order comes from part order and the sibling priority tiebreak; no global state.
- **Debug drawing:** nothing ragdoll-specific; `SkeletonPose::Draw` and the constraint draws are under `JPH_DEBUG_RENDERER`, off; every input is public.
- **Against the decided tickets (51 to 54, 56):** consistent. One combined private filter (ticket 51) is what the filter rule demands; ordinary bodies and joints (ADR-0053) lose nothing, since every `Ragdoll` method is a loop FenrirRagdoll writes; following with kinematic bodies and a motion-type switch (ADR-0054) is what the host does in any case. Worth the spec's attention: `Stabilize` is mass and inertia shaping, not solver tuning, so the derivation (ticket 53) may clamp parent–child mass ratios itself as plain data; the spec's default limits can start from `sCreate`'s cones and `Human.tof`'s motors (20 Hz, damping 2, ±1000 N m); and `PositionAndVelocity` motors are the listed path to target velocities.
