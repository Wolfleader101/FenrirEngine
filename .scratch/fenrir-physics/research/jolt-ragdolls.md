## 1. `RagdollSettings` and `Ragdoll`

**What a part is.** `RagdollSettings::Part` *is a* `BodyCreationSettings` plus one `Ref<TwoBodyConstraintSettings> mToParent` ([Ragdoll.h L89-L96](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L89-L96)). `mParts` is "1-on-1 with mSkeleton.GetJoints()" ([L121-L122](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L121-L122)): one body per bone, and the joint to the parent bone's body, null for the root. `mAdditionalConstraints` holds `{ int mBodyIdx[2]; Ref<TwoBodyConstraintSettings> }` for joints that are not parent-child ([L101-L116, L125](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L101-L116); added in a past release, [ReleaseNotes.md L396](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L396)). Any `TwoBodyConstraintSettings` kind is accepted at this level; the loader's override table builds fixed, point, hinge, slider, cone and swing-twist parts (section 8).

**`CreateRagdoll(groupID, userData, system)`** ([Ragdoll.cpp L407-L455](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L407-L455)):
- Calls `BodyInterface::CreateBody` per part, in part order, through the **locking** body interface (L415-L420). Returns `nullptr` and deletes the half-built ragdoll if Jolt runs out of body IDs (L421-L426).
- Sets every body's `CollisionGroup::mGroupID` to `groupID` and its user data to `userData` (L427-L428). The group filter and sub-group come from the part's `mCollisionGroup` (section 2).
- Creates each part's joint with `mToParent->Create(*parentBody, *thisBody)`: the **parent is body 1, the child body 2** (L434-L438). Then the additional joints (L446-L452).
- Creates nothing in the `PhysicsSystem`: bodies and joints exist but are not added. The result holds `RefConst<RagdollSettings>`, `Array<BodyID>` and `Array<Ref<TwoBodyConstraint>>` ([Ragdoll.h L239-L245](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L239-L245)).

**Only from settings.** `Ragdoll`'s members are private and only `RagdollSettings` is a friend ([Ragdoll.h L234-L245](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L234-L245)); the public constructor takes only the system ([L142](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L142)) and there is no setter for body IDs or joints. A `Ragdoll` cannot be wrapped around bodies and joints that already exist.

**`AddToPhysicsSystem(activation, lockBodies)`** ([Ragdoll.cpp L501-L518](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L501-L518)): copies the body IDs (because `AddBodiesPrepare` shuffles its array, L505-L508; [BodyInterface.h L123](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.h#L123)), adds the bodies as one batch (`AddBodiesPrepare`/`AddBodiesFinalize`, L511-L513), then `AddConstraints` as one batch (L517). The batch add is what Jolt recommends over one body at a time, which "results in a really inefficient broadphase until ... Update rebuilds the tree" ([BodyInterface.h L96-L98](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.h#L96-L98)). FenrirPhysics already batches its bodies the same way ([JoltBackend.cpp L1638-L1640](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)).

**`RemoveFromPhysicsSystem(lockBodies)`** ([L520-L535](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L520-L535)): `RemoveConstraints` first, then `RemoveBodies` as a batch, which deactivates them and takes them out of the broad phase ([BodyInterface.cpp L202-L211](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L202-L211)). Bodies and joints survive, so the ragdoll can be added again. **`~Ragdoll` destroys the bodies** (`DestroyBodies`, [L495-L499](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L495-L499)) but does not remove them: every sample removes in its destructor before the `Ref` goes ([CreateRigTest.cpp L17-L20](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/CreateRigTest.cpp#L17-L20)). The joints are released with the `Ref`s *(inference)*.

So, against adding one at a time: the two methods give the batch broad-phase insert, the joints-before-bodies removal order FenrirPhysics already keeps, and nothing else.

**`Stabilize`** ([Ragdoll.cpp L51-L190](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L51-L190)), "Based on: Stop my Constraints from Blowing Up! - Oliver Strunk (Havok)" (L53). Despite the header's "Stabilize the constraints" ([Ragdoll.h L26](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L26)), it touches no joint. It rewrites the parts' **mass properties**:
- Every part with mass gets `mOverrideMassProperties = MassAndInertiaProvided` with its computed mass properties (L73-L78), so the shape's density stops mattering afterwards.
- Within each chain of movable parts (a static part starts a new chain, L62-L85), the child:parent mass ratio is clamped to [0.8, 1.2] and the chain's total mass redistributed to keep that total (L108-L142).
- Each part's principal inertia is raised to at least `min(2 x own, sum of children's)` (L144-L185). Fails (returns false) only if an inertia tensor cannot be decomposed (L156-L159).

It works on the settings before `CreateRagdoll`, and only on `mParts`; it is not usable on bodies or joints created separately. Its effect, mass and inertia overrides, could be reproduced on any `BodyCreationSettings` list that has a parent index per entry *(inference)*.

**`CalculateConstraintPriorities(base)`** ([L192-L222](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L192-L222)): asserts `AreJointsCorrectlyOrdered` (L195), gives each part `base + its longest distance to a leaf` (the root gets the largest number, leaves `base`, L197-L212), copies that to each part's `mToParent->mConstraintPriority` (L215-L217) and gives an additional joint the lower of its two bodies' values (L219-L221). Jolt sorts an island's joints by priority ascending, then by their index in the constraint list ([ConstraintManager.cpp L103-L115](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ConstraintManager.cpp#L103-L115)); "Higher numbers are more likely to be solved correctly" ([Constraint.h L80-L82](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/Constraint.h#L80-L82)), so the root's joints are solved last. Siblings share a value, so their order falls back to the list index, which changes on removal ([ConstraintManager.cpp L45-L54](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ConstraintManager.cpp#L45-L54)). Added in the release notes as a boost "towards the root" ([ReleaseNotes.md L58](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L58)).
- **FenrirPhysics's priority** is the joint entity (`desc.priority = entity`, [Joints.cpp L464](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Joints.cpp); [JoltBackend.cpp L1765-L1767](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)). It gives the same determinism (unique values, so the index tiebreak is never reached) but not the same order: a ragdoll's root joints are solved last only if their entities sort last. The two cannot both hold on one `mConstraintPriority` *(inference)*.

**Lookup tables.** `CalculateBodyIndexToConstraintIndex` and `CalculateConstraintIndexToBodyIdxPair` fill the maps `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` needs ([Ragdoll.h L68-L87](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L68-L87); [Ragdoll.cpp L457-L493](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L457-L493)). `CreateRagdoll` does not call them; the loader does ([RagdollLoader.cpp L118-L120, L308-L309](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.cpp#L118-L120)) and the binary restore does ([Ragdoll.cpp L399-L401](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L399-L401)). The first map "will not look in the additional constraint list" ([Ragdoll.h L72](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L72)).

## 2. Collision inside a ragdoll

**`DisableParentChildCollisions(jointMatrices, minSeparation)`** ([Ragdoll.cpp L224-L289](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L224-L289); doc at [Ragdoll.h L35-L47](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L35-L47)):
- Builds one `GroupFilterTable(jointCount)` and disables each (bone, parent) pair (L230-L236).
- With a pose, for every pair still enabled it runs `CollisionDispatch::sCollideShapeVsShape` on the two shapes at their centre-of-mass transforms, with `mMaxSeparationDistance = minSeparation`, back faces and all edges on, and disables the pair on any hit (L238-L280). Only pairs where at least one part has mass are tested (L267), and the shape with mass goes first so convex-vs-mesh works (L271-L274). So overlapping non-adjacent parts in the given pose (a shoulder against a torso) never collide.
- Gives every part `SubGroupID = bone index` and the shared filter (L282-L288). The `GroupID` comes later, per instance, from `CreateRagdoll`; the doc wants it "unique throughout the PhysicsSystem" ([Ragdoll.h L45-L46](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L45-L46)). All ragdolls from one settings object share the one table *(inference, one `Ref` per settings)*. `sCreate` calls it without a pose ([RagdollLoader.cpp L306](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.cpp#L306)); `Human.tof` ships its table in the file (23 sub-groups, 32 bytes, [Human.tof L629-L635](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Assets/Human.tof?plain=1#L629-L635)).

**`GroupFilterTable`** ([GroupFilterTable.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Collision/GroupFilterTable.h)): a bit per sub-group pair, `N(N-1)/2` bits (L12-L34), all enabled at construction (L62-L68). Its rules, in order (L96-L116):
- either group is `cInvalidGroup`: collide;
- different `GroupID`s: collide (so two ragdolls from one settings object collide with each other);
- same group but **different filter objects: do not collide**;
- same sub-group: do not collide;
- otherwise the table bit.

**What it costs.**
- Memory: `N(N-1)/2` bits rounded up to bytes, shared by every instance of the settings (23 sub-groups is 32 bytes).
- Per candidate pair: one virtual `CanCollide`, up to four integer compares and one bit test; no allocation and no lock. The pose-based scan in `DisableParentChildCollisions` is a one-off `O(N^2)` narrow-phase test at settings time, never at run time.
- `CollisionGroup` holds a `RefConst<GroupFilter>` ([CollisionGroup.h L104](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Collision/CollisionGroup.h#L104)), so each body adds a reference to the table and a `SetCollisionGroup` is a ref-count change.

**Where Jolt asks a group filter.** `CollisionGroup::CanCollide` asks "the first group filter that's not null" of the two bodies ([CollisionGroup.h L15-L18, L83-L92](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Collision/CollisionGroup.h#L83-L92)). It runs in `Body::sFindCollidingPairsCanCollide` during broad-phase pair finding, after the motion-type and active-index checks ([Body.inl L30-L77](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.inl#L30-L77); called from [QuadTree.cpp L1477](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Collision/BroadPhase/QuadTree.cpp#L1477)) and in the CCD cast ([PhysicsSystem.cpp L1991-L1993](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L1991-L1993)).

**Against object layers and queries** ([Architecture.md L648-L653](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/Architecture.md?plain=1#L648-L653)):
- The pipeline is broad-phase layer, then object layer pair, then group filter, then shape filter, then contact listener. A group filter can only remove a pair the layer rule allowed; it cannot add one.
- The group filter is "Used only during simulation"; `CastRay`, `CollideShape` and the other queries use `BodyFilter` instead. A ragdoll's own parts are therefore hit by casts and overlaps whatever their group says, and a query cannot be told "skip this ragdoll" through its group; that needs a `BodyFilter` over its body IDs or a layer *(inference)*.
- Sensors are unaffected: the group test runs before the sensor test in CCD ([PhysicsSystem.cpp L1991-L1996](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L1991-L1996)) and a ragdoll part in a sensor's layer pair still triggers it unless the group says no *(inference)*.
- This is the same picture FenrirPhysics's joints ticket records for `collideConnected`: the joint only removes contact the layer rule allows, and queries are unaffected.

**One group per body.** `Body` holds a single `CollisionGroup` ([Body.h L454](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.h#L454)) with one `RefConst<GroupFilter>`, one `GroupID` and one `SubGroupID` ([CollisionGroup.h L104-L106](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Collision/CollisionGroup.h#L104-L106)); `Ragdoll::SetGroupID` rewrites the ID on every body and keeps filter and sub-group ([Ragdoll.cpp L559-L571](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L559-L571)).
- FenrirPhysics gives a jointed body `CollisionGroup(joinedPairs, bodyIndex, 0)` ([JoltBackend.cpp L1436-L1439](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)) and its `JoinedPairs::CanCollide` returns true when either body's filter is not itself ([L571-L580](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)). A body **cannot** be in that pair table and in a `GroupFilterTable` at once. Mixing the two filters on different bodies makes the answer depend on which body is "first" in the pair, which is the active-body index order ([Body.inl L71](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.inl#L71)): with `JoinedPairs` first the table is never asked *(inference)*.
- Everything the table expresses is a set of body pairs, which the pair table already is: parent-child pairs are the joints' `collideConnected = false`, and the pose-based overlap set is extra pairs *(inference)*. The pose test itself (`sCollideShapeVsShape` with a separation distance) is callable without a `RagdollSettings`.

## 3. Posing

**Space of the joint matrices.** `SetPose(rootOffset, jointMatrices)` calls `SetPositionAndRotation(bodyID, rootOffset + matrix.GetTranslation(), matrix.GetQuaternion(), DontActivate)` per body ([Ragdoll.cpp L580-L589](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L580-L589)).
- `BodyInterface::SetPositionAndRotation` sets the body's **origin**, not its centre of mass: `Body::GetPosition` is `mPosition - mRotation * shape->GetCenterOfMass()` ([Body.h L272-L273](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.h#L272-L273); [BodyInterface.cpp L389-L410](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L389-L410)).
- So **a bone's matrix is its body's origin transform**: the bone frame *is* the body frame, and the shape is placed relative to it. Jolt has no body-to-bone offset anywhere in `Ragdoll`; the offset is baked into the shape.
- `Human.tof` does exactly that, wrapping each capsule in a `StaticCompoundShape` with an offset and rotation from the bone ([Human.tof L611-L627](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Assets/Human.tof?plain=1#L611-L627); 23 compounds over 15 capsules, 8 tapered capsules and 2 boxes). Its joint anchors coincide with the child bone's origin (`mPosition1 == mPosition2 == part.mPosition`, [L703, L706 against L76](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Assets/Human.tof?plain=1#L701-L706)), as a skeleton's do.
- `sCreate` instead puts the body origin at each limb's centre and the joint anchor between limbs ([RagdollLoader.cpp L160-L206](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.cpp#L160-L206)), so its "bones" sit mid-limb and no animation skeleton would match them without an offset.
- Where ADR-0038 and ADR-0040 keep a body-to-bone offset in the glue, Jolt's equivalent is a compound shape and no offset. Both place the collider correctly; only the first keeps the body origin free *(inference)*.
- The `SkeletonPose` overload asserts the pose's skeleton is the settings' and forwards root offset and matrices ([L573-L578](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L573-L578)).
- Matrices are model space relative to the root offset: `CalculateJointMatrices` multiplies parents first and asserts that order ([SkeletonPose.cpp L22-L35](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonPose.cpp#L22-L35)); the root offset is "Extra offset applied to the root" ([SkeletonPose.h L32-L34](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonPose.h#L32-L34)) and carries the `RVec3` for double-precision worlds.
- `SetPose` sets no velocity and does not wake; it notifies the broad phase ([BodyInterface.cpp L398-L401](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L398-L401)). The samples call it before `AddToPhysicsSystem` ([RigPileTest.cpp L133-L135](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/RigPileTest.cpp#L133-L135)) or right after ([PoweredRigTest.cpp L48-L63](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/PoweredRigTest.cpp#L48-L63)).

**`GetPose`** ([L591-L621](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L591-L621)): locks all bodies (`BodyLockMultiRead`), takes body 0's world transform as the root, its translation as the root offset, and every other body's `GetWorldTransform()` (origin space) with the root offset subtracted. The `SkeletonPose` overload fills `GetJointMatrices()` and the root offset but not the local `JointState`s; `CalculateJointStates` does that ([SkeletonPose.cpp L37-L52](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonPose.cpp#L37-L52)).

**`ResetWarmStart`** calls `Constraint::ResetWarmStart` on every joint ([L623-L627](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L623-L627)), which deactivates the accumulated impulses so the next step does not apply last step's ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L354-L360](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L354-L360)). Jolt's doc: use it "when repositioning a ragdoll through Ragdoll::SetPose in such a way that the orientation of the bodies completely changes"; calling it every frame makes joints "much 'softer'" ([Constraint.h L167-L172](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/Constraint.h#L167-L172); [ReleaseNotes.md L316](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L316)). FenrirPhysics already resets warm start on a teleport (joints ticket 26).

## 4. Driving

**`DriveToPoseUsingKinematics(rootOffset, matrices, dt)`** ([Ragdoll.cpp L636-L645](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L636-L645)) calls `BodyInterface::MoveKinematic(body, rootOffset + translation, rotation, dt)` per body: that sets linear velocity `(targetCOM - COM) / dt` and angular velocity from the delta rotation ([Body.cpp L81-L94](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.cpp#L81-L94); [MotionProperties.inl L9-L21](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/MotionProperties.inl#L9-L21)) and wakes the body if the velocity is non-zero and it is in the broad phase ([BodyInterface.cpp L547-L559](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L547-L559)). It needs non-static bodies ([Body.cpp L83](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.cpp#L83)), **not kinematic ones**: `SoftKeyframedRigTest` drives dynamic bodies with it and adds `gravity * dt` to cancel the coming step's gravity ([SoftKeyframedRigTest.cpp L44, L88-L92](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/SoftKeyframedRigTest.cpp#L88-L92)), capping `mMaxLinearVelocity` at 10 m/s to tame the head hitting a bar (L46-L52). Bones with no body are not involved. This is one `MoveKinematic` per body, which FenrirPhysics's backend already exposes ([JoltBackend.cpp L2438-L2441](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)) and its kinematic bodies already use each tick (ADR-0040).

**`DriveToPoseUsingMotors(pose)`** ([L647-L680](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L647-L680)). For each bone with a parent joint (`GetConstraintIndexForBodyIndex`, so never an additional joint, L654):
- reads only the pose's **local `JointState::mRotation`** (L658); translations and the root offset are ignored;
- **swing-twist:** both motors to `Position`, `SetTargetOrientationBS(rotation)` (L663-L669);
- **hinge:** motor to `Position`, `SetTargetOrientationBS(rotation)` (L670-L675);
- **anything else:** `JPH_ASSERT(false, "Constraint type not implemented!")` (L676-L677), a release-build no-op. Hinge support and that assert arrived in later releases ([ReleaseNotes.md L79, L272](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L79)).

"BS" is body space: the target satisfies `R2 = R1 * inOrientation` for the bodies' world rotations, converted into constraint space as `C1^* * q * C2` ([SwingTwistConstraint.h L143-L145](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.h#L143-L145)). This only matches the animation if the body frames are the bone frames (section 3). The constraint-space setter clamps the target into the swing and twist limits ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L186-L197](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L186-L197)). The hinge projects the body-space target onto its hinge axis (`rh1 = inOrientation * mInvInitialOrientation`, then the angle about the axis) and `SetTargetAngle` clamps to the limits ([HingeConstraint.cpp L137-L154](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.cpp#L137-L154); [HingeConstraint.h L123](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.h#L123)). Changing a motor state deactivates its accumulated impulse ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L159-L184](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L159-L184)) and asserts the motor settings are valid.

**`DriveToPoseUsingMotors(prevPose, pose, dt)`** ([L682-L724](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L682-L724); [ReleaseNotes.md L25](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L25)): the target angular velocity is `(rotation * prevRotation^*).GetAngularVelocity(dt)` in parent-body space (L699); motors go to `PositionAndVelocity`; swing-twist gets it rotated into body 2 space with `R2^* * R1` **read from the live bodies** (L709-L710), the hinge gets its dot with the local hinge axis (L717). Asserts `dt > 0` (L686).

**Where strength lives.** In each joint's `MotorSettings`, never in the `Ragdoll`:
- `MotorSettings` is a `SpringSettings` (default `FrequencyAndDamping`, 2 Hz, damping 1) plus force and torque limits (default +-`FLT_MAX`); `SetTorqueLimit(t)` sets the symmetric pair and `IsValid` wants non-negative frequency and damping and min <= max ([MotorSettings.h L39-L80](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/MotorSettings.h#L39-L80)).
- A swing-twist joint has separate swing and twist motor settings and one friction torque, all writable live through references ([SwingTwistConstraint.h L115-L122](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.h#L115-L122)); a hinge has one motor and one friction torque ([HingeConstraint.h L111-L116](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.h#L111-L116)).
- A `Position` motor does nothing unless the spring has stiffness (frequency > 0); `PositionAndVelocity` needs stiffness or damping ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L270-L305](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L270-L305); [HingeConstraint.cpp L221-L229](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.cpp#L221-L229); [SpringSettings.h L41-L44](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SpringSettings.h#L41-L44)).
- The torque limit is applied per step as an impulse limit `dt * limit` on each motor axis ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L387-L388, L409-L410](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L387-L388); [HingeConstraint.cpp L297](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.cpp#L297)). The swing motor shares one limit across its two axes.
- Friction (`mMaxFrictionTorque`) is the motor parts with zero target velocity while the motor is off ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L255-L266, L311-L321](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L255-L266)): a limp ragdoll's joint damping lives here.
- The motor doc is [Architecture.md L493-L551](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/Architecture.md?plain=1#L493-L551): `force = stiffness * (target - current) + damping * (targetVelocity - velocity)`, three spring modes (frequency, stiffness, mass-normalised stiffness), and the advice that unlimited torque "lets the motor achieve its target as fast as possible".
- The spring frequency is per joint, not per ragdoll, so stiff and limp limbs on one body are a per-joint edit, which FenrirPhysics's `Joint` components already are *(inference)*.

**Reproducible without `Ragdoll`?** Yes, exactly: FenrirPhysics's `SwingTwistMotor` already has `Position` mode, a `targetRotation` ("frameB's rotation relative to frameA"), a `maxTorque` and a `spring` ([Joints.hpp L78-L91](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/include/FenrirPhysics/Joints.hpp)), and the backend applies it with the same calls (`SetSwingMotorState`, `SetTwistMotorState`, `SetTargetOrientationCS`, both motor settings from one spring and torque, [JoltBackend.cpp L417-L432](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)). `JointMotor` on a hinge has `targetPosition` ([Joints.hpp L65-L76](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/include/FenrirPhysics/Joints.hpp)). What is missing against Jolt's loop: a `PositionAndVelocity` mode (FenrirPhysics's `JointMotorMode` has Off, Velocity, Position), the body-space to constraint-space conversion (Jolt's `SetTargetOrientationBS` does it from the private `mConstraintToBody1/2`; FenrirPhysics would compute it from its own frames), and the per-bone loop itself, which is a glue concern *(inference)*.

## 6. Skeleton helpers

**`Skeleton`** ([Skeleton.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/Skeleton.h)): an array of `Joint { String mName; String mParentName; int mParentJointIndex = -1; }` (L25-L36).
- `AddJoint(name, parentName)` or `AddJoint(name, parentIndex)` (L47-L48); the index form fills the parent name from the parent.
- `GetJointIndex(name)` is a linear search ([Skeleton.cpp L25-L32](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/Skeleton.cpp#L25-L32)). Names are plain strings with no uniqueness rule; a duplicate resolves to the first (L27-L30).
- `CalculateParentJointIndices` resolves parent names to indices (L34-L38) and must be called after loading a text asset, because the text stream stores names only ([Skeleton.cpp L14-L18](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/Skeleton.cpp#L14-L18); [LoadSaveRigTest.cpp L55-L56](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/LoadSaveRigTest.cpp#L55-L56)). The binary form stores both (L49-L57).
- **`AreJointsCorrectlyOrdered`** is `parentIndex < ownIndex` for every bone (L40-L47). It is what "parents first" means to Jolt, and `CalculateConstraintPriorities`, `CalculateJointMatrices`, the mapper's lock code and `Map` assert it. There is no reorder helper; the asset must come ordered.
- FenrirSkeleton's `SkeletonData` carries the same facts (name, parent, parents-first order) and more (engine names, ambiguity, `IsDescendant`).

**`SkeletonPose`** ([SkeletonPose.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonPose.h)): a `RefConst<Skeleton>`, an `RVec3` root offset, local `JointState`s (rotation and translation relative to the parent) and model-space `Mat44`s (L75-L79).
- `SetSkeleton` sizes both arrays to the bone count ([SkeletonPose.cpp L14-L20](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonPose.cpp#L14-L20)).
- `CalculateJointMatrices`: locals to model space, parents first (L22-L35).
- `CalculateJointStates`: the inverse, through `Inversed()` of the parent matrix (L37-L52).
- `CalculateLocalSpaceJointMatrices`: the locals as matrices without the hierarchy (L54-L58), which `Map` consumes.
- Nothing here is bind-pose aware or scaled; `JointState::ToMatrix` is rotation and translation only ([SkeletalAnimation.h L29-L36](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletalAnimation.h#L29-L36)). FenrirSkeleton's model matrices are the same idea with scale.

**`SkeletalAnimation`** ([SkeletalAnimation.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletalAnimation.h)):
- Per named bone a list of `Keyframe { JointState; float mTime }` (L39-L58) and one looping flag (L68-L70).
- `GetDuration` is the **first** channel's last key time ([SkeletalAnimation.cpp L47-L53](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletalAnimation.cpp#L47-L53)); `ScaleJoints` scales translations only (L55-L60).
- `Sample(time, pose)`: `fmod` when looping, binary search per channel, lerp translation, slerp rotation, clamp outside the keys (L62-L110). Each call resolves every channel by name through `GetJointIndex` (L82), so a sample is `O(channels x bones)` in name compares.
- It is a sample-asset format, not an animation system: no blending, events, masks or root motion. FenrirAnimation supersedes it entirely for FenrirEngine; the only reason to meet it is reading Jolt's `.tof` clips, which also needs `JPH_OBJECT_STREAM`.

**`SkeletonMapper`** ([SkeletonMapper.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonMapper.h); [ReleaseNotes.md L461](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/ReleaseNotes.md?plain=1#L461)): "map a low detail (ragdoll) skeleton to a high detail (animation) skeleton and vice versa" (L12). Skeleton 1 is the ragdoll's, skeleton 2 the animation's; `Initialize` asserts `n1 <= n2` ([SkeletonMapper.cpp L18](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Skeleton/SkeletonMapper.cpp#L18)) and assumes every ragdoll bone maps to an animation bone (L73-L75 in the header).
- **Mappings:** for each bone of 1, the first bone of 2 that `CanMapJoint` accepts, by default equal names (L64-L71). The mapping stores `mJoint1To2 = neutral1^-1 * neutral2` from the two **model-space neutral poses**, so the two skeletons need not share frames, only positions "as closely as possible" (L76-L81; cpp L24-L39).
- **Chains:** between two mapped bones of 2 with unmapped bones between them, the longest such chain and its counterpart in 1 (cpp L43-L104).
- **Unmapped:** every bone of 2 that is in no mapping or chain (cpp L106-L109).
- **Locked translations:** `LockTranslations(skeleton2, flags, neutral2)` stores each flagged bone's neutral local translation (cpp L112-L131); `LockAllTranslations` flags everything below the first mapped bone, excluding that root because it "determines the position of the entire ragdoll" (cpp L133-L163). The header explains why: "constraints are never 100% rigid, there's always a little bit of stretch in the ragdoll when the ragdoll is under stress" (L83-L86), and locking trades that for a mismatch with the simulation. `SkeletonMapperTest` ships it off by default ([SkeletonMapperTest.h L44](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/SkeletonMapperTest.h#L44)).
- **`Map(pose1Model, pose2Local, outPose2Model)`** (cpp L165-L210): mapped bones copy `pose1 * mJoint1To2`; a chain's start is re-aimed by `Quat::sFromTo` so the chain, run through its local transforms, ends where skeleton 1's end bone is, then the inner bones follow their locals (L171-L195); unmapped bones take their local pose under their (already written) parent (L197-L205); locked translations are overwritten last (L207-L209). The doc warns "very extreme animation poses will show artifacts" (header L100-L103).
- **`MapReverse(pose2Model, outPose1Model)`** uses mappings only (cpp L212-L217).
- The sample does both directions every frame: `MapReverse` then `CalculateJointStates` to feed `DriveToPoseUsingMotors`, and `GetPose` plus the animation's locals through `Map` to get a full animation-skeleton pose back ([SkeletonMapperTest.cpp L91-L135](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/SkeletonMapperTest.cpp#L91-L135)).

**Against the seam.** The mapper is pure matrix code over two `Skeleton`s and `Mat44` arrays; it never touches a body, a constraint or the `PhysicsSystem`. Its inputs (names, parents, neutral poses) are what FenrirSkeleton's `SkeletonData` and base pose already hold, and its forward direction is what ADR-0038's `PoseOverride` world targets already do for mapped bones, with unmapped bones keeping their animated local pose (ADR-0038's "Bones with no body keep their animated local pose"). What ADR-0038 does not do is the chain re-aiming or the translation locking; both are glue-side policies over bone matrices *(inference)*.

## 7. Kinematic and dynamic switching

**What the samples do.** None switches a ragdoll's motion type at run time. Each picks a mode at load:
- `KinematicRigTest` loads with `EMotionType::Kinematic` (the loader overwrites every part's `mMotionType`, [RagdollLoader.cpp L32-L35](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.cpp#L32-L35)), poses once with `SetPose`, then each frame samples the clip and calls `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics(pose, dt)` ([KinematicRigTest.cpp L45-L62, L65-L82](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/KinematicRigTest.cpp#L45-L62)). It "demonstrates that kinematic bodies don't get influenced by static bodies" ([KinematicRigTest.h L22-L23](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/KinematicRigTest.h#L22-L23)); `Samples.md` calls it "infinite mass, simulated using velocities only" ([L58](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/Samples.md?plain=1#L58)).
- `PoweredRigTest` loads `Dynamic` and calls one of the two `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` each frame, after moving the clip root's translation into the pose's root offset taken from body 0 ([PoweredRigTest.cpp L45, L66-L115](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/PoweredRigTest.cpp#L66-L115)). It draws the average and maximum distance between last frame's target and each joint's current anchor on body 2 (L93-L109).
- `SoftKeyframedRigTest` loads `Dynamic` and uses `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` plus the gravity cancel (section 4), "Since the bodies are dynamic, they will collide with static objects" ([SoftKeyframedRigTest.h L22-L24](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/SoftKeyframedRigTest.h#L22-L24)).

So Jolt's three ways to follow an animation are: kinematic bodies with velocities, dynamic bodies with velocities, dynamic bodies with motors. The switch between following and simulating is the host's.

**What Jolt requires of a motion-type change.**
- The body needs `MotionProperties`: created non-static or with `mAllowDynamicOrKinematic` ([Body.cpp L42](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.cpp#L42); [BodyCreationSettings.h L56, L99](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyCreationSettings.h#L99)). `Human.tof` has that flag false but is loaded Kinematic or Dynamic, so its bodies can switch ([Human.tof L63](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Assets/Human.tof?plain=1#L63)).
- To Static the body must be inactive first (L43); `BodyInterface::SetMotionType` deactivates it for you and activates on request ([BodyInterface.cpp L858-L875](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L858-L875)). To Kinematic resets force and torque, keeps velocities; to Static zeroes velocities (L56-L66).
- A two-body joint is active only while one body is active **and one is dynamic** ([TwoBodyConstraint.h L38](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/TwoBodyConstraint.h#L38)); the island builder links the bodies only if both are dynamic and asserts at least one is ([TwoBodyConstraint.cpp L31-L55](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/TwoBodyConstraint.cpp#L31-L55)). A fully kinematic ragdoll's joints are therefore skipped, not solved: they cost nothing and correct nothing, so a kinematic pose may violate its limits *(inference)*. A partly kinematic ragdoll (hips kinematic, limbs dynamic) is a valid tree: each limb joint has one dynamic body.
- The constraint itself needs nothing on a switch; FenrirPhysics's joints ticket 26 already keeps the constraint across motion-type changes, and its `SetMotion` uses `SetMotionType(..., Activate)` plus a broad-phase relayer ([JoltBackend.cpp L2492-L2497](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/Backend/Jolt/JoltBackend.cpp)).
- `Stabilize`'s mass rewrite is for dynamic parts; a kinematic body's mass is never used ([Body.inl L36-L44](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.inl#L36-L44) decides pairs by motion type, not mass).
- Jolt's one timing rule for a body following a pose: a position write on a sleeping body must wake it, which `BodyInterface::MoveKinematic` does ([Body.h L223-L225](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.h#L223-L225)). `Body::AddRotationStep` integrates rotation by axis-angle "for kinematic ragdolls that are driven to a new pose if the poses differ enough" ([Body.inl L82-L89](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.inl#L82-L89)).

## 9. Threading, timing and determinism

**Locking.** Every `Ragdoll` call that touches bodies takes `inLockBodies` (default true) and picks `GetBodyInterface()` or `GetBodyInterfaceNoLock()` accordingly ([Ragdoll.h L148-L229](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h#L148-L229); [Ragdoll.cpp L41-L49](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L41-L49)).

| Call | Locks taken with `inLockBodies = true` | Also touches |
|---|---|---|
| `AddToPhysicsSystem` | all bodies at once (`BodyLockMultiWrite` in `AddBodiesFinalize`), broad-phase modification lock, constraint list mutex | activation |
| `RemoveFromPhysicsSystem` | constraint list mutex, then all bodies at once | deactivation, broad phase |
| `Activate`, `SetLinearAndAngularVelocity`, `SetLinearVelocity`, `AddLinearVelocity`, `AddImpulse`, `SetPose`, `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` | one body at a time through `BodyInterface` | `SetPose` notifies the broad phase; `MoveKinematic` may activate |
| `IsActive`, `GetPose`, `GetWorldSpaceBounds` | all bodies at once, read | - |
| `SetGroupID` | all bodies at once, write | - |
| `GetRootTransform` | body 0, read | - |
| `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` (both), `ResetWarmStart` | **no lock flag, no lock**: raw joint and body pointers | joints' motor state and targets |

Sources: [Ragdoll.cpp L501-L785](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L501-L785); [BodyInterface.cpp L185-L211](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.cpp#L185-L211); [ConstraintManager.cpp L17-L19](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ConstraintManager.cpp#L17-L19).

`PhysicsSystem::Update` holds every body lock and, from the island build, every constraint lock for the step ([PhysicsSystem.cpp L235-L236, L334, L664-L672](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L235-L236)), so the locking variants cannot run during a step; the no-lock variants exist for step listeners, where Jolt suggests to "set velocities on ragdoll bodies" ([Architecture.md L928](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/Architecture.md?plain=1#L928)). A world with no active bodies still runs `Update`'s early-out only if there is no step listener ([PhysicsSystem.cpp L188-L191](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L188-L191)); a ragdoll adds none.

**Unlocked writes.** `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` has no lock flag: it writes the joints and, in the velocity overload, reads both bodies' rotations through the raw `Body` pointers ([Ragdoll.cpp L709](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L709)). Jolt's rule: joints "can be freely modified except during the physics simulation step" and the owner "ensures that it only modifies its own constraints" ([Architecture.md L489](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Docs/Architecture.md?plain=1#L489)). `ResetWarmStart` is the same. `AddToPhysicsSystem` and `RemoveFromPhysicsSystem` take the constraint list mutex ([ConstraintManager.cpp L17-L19](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ConstraintManager.cpp#L17-L19)) and the body locks of the batch, so they are between-step calls too.

**Against FenrirPhysics's tick.** Its step runs joints, pushes and character moves, then `m_backend.Step`, then write-back and joint measures ([PhysicsWorld.cpp L98-L135](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/src/PhysicsWorld.cpp)). Every `Ragdoll` call above is a between-step call, so each maps onto what FenrirPhysics already does before or after `Step` *(inference)*.

**Order-dependence.** `CreateRagdoll` creates bodies in part order and joints in part order then additional order ([Ragdoll.cpp L418-L452](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L418-L452)); `AddConstraints` appends in that order ([ConstraintManager.cpp L22-L34](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ConstraintManager.cpp#L22-L34)), which fixes the index tiebreak between equal priorities (section 1) until any constraint anywhere is removed. Body IDs follow creation order, and `sFindCollidingPairsCanCollide` orders pairs by active-body index ([Body.inl L55-L71](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Body/Body.inl#L55-L71)), which decides whose group filter is asked (section 2). Nothing in `Ragdoll`, `Skeleton*` or `GroupFilterTable` holds process-global state. `RigPileTest` seeds `default_random_engine` with its default, so its layout is repeatable (L99). None of this needs more than FenrirPhysics's same-machine determinism (`CROSS_PLATFORM_DETERMINISTIC OFF`, [libs/CMakeLists.txt L22](../../../packages/FenrirPhysics/libs/CMakeLists.txt)).

**Save state.** `RagdollSettings` has its own binary save; the runtime `Ragdoll` has none, and its bodies and joints are in `PhysicsSystem::SaveState` like any others *(inference, [Ragdoll.h](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.h) declares no save)*. The samples save only their clock ([PoweredRigTest.cpp L129-L137](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Tests/Rig/PoweredRigTest.cpp#L129-L137)).

## 10. Debug drawing

`Ragdoll.h` and `Ragdoll.cpp` contain no `JPH_DEBUG_RENDERER` code at all; a ragdoll draws through its bodies and joints like any others. Ragdoll-adjacent drawing is:

- **Swing-twist joints:** `DrawConstraint` draws body 1's frame and the current swing and twist axes, `DrawConstraintLimits` the swing cone or pyramid and a twist pie ([SwingTwistConstraint.cpp L446-L492](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/SwingTwistConstraint.cpp#L446-L492)); hinges draw their axes and a limit pie ([HingeConstraint.cpp L361-L391](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/HingeConstraint.cpp#L361-L391)). `mDrawConstraintSize` is 0.1 in both sample rigs ([RagdollLoader.cpp L287](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Samples/Utils/RagdollLoader.cpp#L287); [Human.tof L702](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics/blob/v5.6.0/Assets/Human.tof?plain=1#L702)).

## What this means for FenrirPhysics

Facts the ragdoll spec will rest on. These are not the decisions themselves.

**What the `Ragdoll` class is:**
- A container of one `BodyID` per bone and one `Ref<TwoBodyConstraint>` per non-root bone plus extras, buildable **only** from a `RagdollSettings`, never around existing bodies and joints.
- Every runtime method is a loop over those bodies through `BodyInterface`, or a per-joint motor state and target write. None holds state the bodies and joints do not.
- The settings-side helpers, `Stabilize` (mass and inertia rebalancing), `CalculateConstraintPriorities` (root solved last) and `DisableParentChildCollisions` (a pair table plus a pose-based overlap scan), are each a function over the part list and skeleton that FenrirPhysics could run over its own descriptions, with or without Jolt's classes.

**Where it meets the seam:**
- FenrirPhysics already creates bodies in a batch, removes joints before bodies, exposes `MoveKinematic`, resets warm start on teleport, keeps joints across motion-type changes and drives swing-twist and hinge motors to a target rotation or angle. The only Jolt motor feature it lacks is `PositionAndVelocity`.
- A body carries one `CollisionGroup`; FenrirPhysics's pair-table `GroupFilter` and Jolt's `GroupFilterTable` cannot share a body and should not share a world. A ragdoll's non-collision is a set of body pairs, which the pair table already represents.
- `mConstraintPriority` is one number: either the joint entity (today's determinism) or the tree depth (Jolt's root-last), not both.

**Posing and driving:**
- Jolt's pose contract is "bone matrix = body origin transform", with the shape offset inside the body. Motor targets are body-space rotations of the child relative to the parent, which only equal the animation's local rotations under that contract.
- Three following modes exist in the samples: kinematic bodies with velocities, dynamic bodies with velocities plus a gravity cancel, dynamic bodies with motors. None switches at run time; the switch is the host's `SetMotionType` plus whatever it does with velocities and targets.
- A joint between two kinematic bodies is inactive and never solved.

**Skeleton helpers:**
- `Skeleton`, `SkeletonPose` and `SkeletalAnimation` are sample-grade asset classes that FenrirSkeleton and FenrirAnimation already exceed. `SkeletonMapper` is pure matrix code over names, parents and two neutral poses; its forward direction is ADR-0038's override write with unmapped bones keeping their locals, and its extras (chain re-aiming, translation locking) are policies over bone matrices.

**Sample defaults worth carrying forward:**
- A 12-part capsule human with swing-twist everywhere, elbows and knees as zero-normal-cone hinges with +-45 deg twist, spine 5 deg twist and 10 deg cones, head 90 deg twist and 45 deg cones, shoulders 45/90/45, hips 45/45/45.
- The 23-part asset's motors: 20 Hz, damping 2, +-1000 N m per axis, 5 N m friction; bodies at friction 1, restitution 0.15, damping 0.05.
- 160 ragdolls is the pile sample's ceiling, each with a unique group ID, motors holding a death pose.

**Options the spec can weigh (not chosen here):**
1. Build ragdolls entirely from existing `RigidBody` and `Joint` entities, and port the three settings-side helpers (mass rebalancing, root-last priority, pose-based pair exclusion) as FenrirPhysics functions over its own description. Nothing of Jolt's `Ragdoll` class crosses the seam.
2. Keep a `Ragdoll` per ragdoll behind the seam for `SetPose`/`GetPose`/`DriveToPose*` batching. Requires creating the bodies and joints through `RagdollSettings`, so the backend would own creation for ragdoll parts differently from other bodies, and the pair table would have to coexist with `GroupFilterTable` on different bodies, which the filter rule makes order-dependent.
3. Something between: FenrirPhysics builds a `RagdollSettings` privately only to call `Stabilize` and `DisableParentChildCollisions(pose)`, copies the results (mass overrides, disabled pairs) into its own bodies and pair table, and discards it.
4. For the priority: keep the entity-based priority, or give ragdoll joints a depth-based block of priorities with a per-ragdoll base, or leave root-last to the solver's iteration count.
5. For following: expose `PositionAndVelocity` on `JointMotor` and `SwingTwistMotor` so a powered ragdoll can take a target velocity from two consecutive base poses, or leave `Position` with a stiffer spring.

**Surprises worth carrying forward:**
- `Stabilize` is documented as stabilising constraints but rewrites masses and inertias, and converts every part to `MassAndInertiaProvided`.
- `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` silently ignores (release) or asserts on (debug) any parent joint that is not swing-twist or hinge, and never drives additional joints.
- `DriveToPoseUsingKinematics` works on dynamic bodies; it is `MoveKinematic`, not a motion-type change.
- `~Ragdoll` destroys bodies without removing them; the samples always `RemoveFromPhysicsSystem` first.
- `CreateRagdoll` does not build the body-to-joint map that `DriveToPoseUsingMotors` indexes; the loader does.
- `SkeletalAnimation::GetDuration` is the first channel's last key, and `Sample` resolves every channel by a linear name search each call.
- The sample loader's "ragdoll" constraint variant is the swing-twist one; there is no six-DOF variant.
