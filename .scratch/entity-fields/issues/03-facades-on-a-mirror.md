# 03: Facade parts on a mirror generate `Entity`'s native-backed members (optional)

**What to build:** the generator's facade parts (README "Facade parts", `@facade Class.Method` in a native's C++ doc) extended so a mark may name a mirror, static or instance, and `Entity`'s five native-backed members come from `Bindings.cpp` (spec: "Could `Entity` be generated whole?"). Optional: it removes five short hand-written methods for one generator rule; the user decides whether it is worth it after ticket 02.

- **`packages/Iris/Tools/Iris.Bindings`**: a `@facade` mark may name a mirror this binding generates (the refusal "named as a mirror" lifts for a mirror of the binding's own description). `@facade Entity.Create` on a native with no entity parameter is a `public static` method on the mirror's generated part, as on a facade class. `@facade Entity.Destroy(value)` names a field of the mirror: the native's first parameter of that field's C# type (through the `EntityValue` wrapper, `Entity`) is bound to the field, and the method is an instance method on the mirror (`public unsafe Result Destroy() => Native.EntityDestroy(value);`, the declaration's doc kept). A mark naming a field the mirror does not have, or a native whose first parameter is not that field's type, is refused naming both. README and `tests/BindingTests.cs` cases (static on a mirror, instance with a field, the two refusals), with marks on the fixture's natives (`tests/Fixture/Natives.cpp`, re-described into `Fixture.natives.json`) and the fixture regenerated.
- **`packages/FenrirScripting/src/Bindings.cpp`**: `EntityCreate` carries `@facade Entity.Create`, `EntityDestroy` `@facade Entity.Destroy(value)`, `EntityIsValid` `@facade Entity.IsValid(value)`, and the two transform natives likewise; `Entity.cs` keeps what wraps a result (`Create`'s throw on `uint.MaxValue` becomes a hand-written `Create` over a generated `TryCreate`, or the mark is left off `EntityCreate`; the ticket decides by what reads best) and drops what the generated part now gives. Regenerate the natives description and the binding.
