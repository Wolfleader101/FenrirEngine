# 08: Pushing, weight and kinematic shoves

**What to build:** A character pushes light boxes and not heavy crates, tips a seesaw and sinks a raft with its weight, is never pushed by loose bodies, and is shoved by doors and paddles at their own speed (spec: "FenrirPhysics: other bodies and the inner body" (pushing, standing on bodies, dynamic bodies never push, kinematic bodies shove), "the Jolt backend" (pushing and weight)).

- **Pushing:** a move that hits a dynamic body gives it an impulse towards the character's speed at the contact point, aiming at 0.9 × the closing speed, clamped by `pushForce` and never downward; `pushForce = 0` pushes nothing. Static and kinematic bodies are never pushed. Applied in step 3b, acting in this tick's step. No push reading in `state`.
- **Weight:** each move that ends on ground presses the ground body with `mass × gravity × dt` at the ground point (the world's gravity, scaled by how the ground normal faces it); `mass = 0` turns it off. The world's gravity is passed to the backend for this only.
- **Dynamic bodies never push a character:** they meet the inner body as an immovable kinematic wall. Knockback is game code writing `velocity`.
- **Kinematic bodies shove** a character at exactly their own speed into it: the move treats them as surfaces moving at their velocity, and that speed shows in the written-back `velocity` until game code overwrites it. Stated limits documented: a fast body may overlap for a tick; crushing is not detected.
- **Stated limit:** a body resting on a character (a box on its head) is not carried; it slips or is pushed out. A jump still pushes it up.
- **Merged with 06 and 07:** `Push` pushes at the velocity the character actually moves at (game code's, plus ridden and carried). Ticket 07's carried-velocity trim used the full trim rule, so a paddle moving into the character went into carried velocity (then cleared on landing) and not into `velocity`; carried velocity now only loses its part going into a surface, down to none, and never takes on a surface's push.
- **DemoApp:** in `CharacterScene.hpp`, `BuildPushBoxes` (east of the wall, x = 10, z = 3.5/5.5/7.5, clear of 07's lift and shuttle; 20 kg on friction 0.2, reset at each route wrap), `BuildSeesaw` (west, hinged at (-6, 0.6, 0), starting east end down), `BuildPaddleLane` (south, z = 11, kinematic, moved in `Tick` by `MovePaddle`), each with its leg; B (`ThrowBall`) and H (`DropBoxOnHead`) added to the smoke keys, and 3 pauses the paddle with 07's platforms. The smoke autopilot does not reach the new legs; P walks them by hand. The ball's `ContactBegin` log is ticket 11's.

**Status:** resolved, but for the DemoApp hand check, which is the user's (built test-first on branch `claude/character-pushing-6d3dfb`). Where the build settled something the ticket left open:

- **Pushes are FenrirPhysics's, not Jolt's.** Jolt's `CharacterVirtual` pushes a body each time a contact is first hit in a collision iteration, and again in its stair and step-forward test moves, so a move starting a push applied two or three times its max strength (a 20 kg crate on friction 0.2 sped up at 13 m/s² for its first tenth of a second, not 3). The character's listener turns Jolt's impulses off (`mCanReceiveImpulses = false`), and after `ExtendedUpdate` the backend's `Push` applies Jolt's own impulse (0.9 × the closing speed at the contact against the move's velocity, plus its penetration push-out, through the body's effective mass at the contact, the downward part cancelled) over the end-of-move contacts, capped by `pushForce × dt` per body over the whole move.
- **Dynamic bodies never push:** the same listener sets `mCanPushCharacter = false` for dynamic bodies, so a ball moving into the character during its move is a still wall there too, not only in the step (before, a 10 m/s ball shoved a standing character 0.28 m). For ticket 07: this includes a dynamic ground body's velocity in the move's constraints, so riding a raft must come from the ground velocity added to the move, as the spec says.
- **Kinematic shoves needed a fix to Jolt's solve:** a standing character's ground constraint, solved first, can leave a 1e-6 m/s trace of push-out upward; Jolt's velocity-reversal check then ends the solve before the kinematic paddle's constraint is reached, so the paddle shoved on alternate ticks (moved 0, then 3 m/s). The listener's `OnContactSolve` snaps a solved velocity within Jolt's own `IsNearZero(1e-8)` to zero; the shove is then exactly the paddle's speed every tick.
- **Weight** is Jolt's: `mass` goes to `SetMass` each move, and the gravity passed to `ExtendedUpdate` is the world's (`PhysicsSystem::GetGravity`), used by Jolt only for that impulse. `CharacterMove` carries `pushForce` and `mass`.
