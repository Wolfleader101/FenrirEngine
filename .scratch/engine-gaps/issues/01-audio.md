# The engine has no audio

Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: none

## What is missing

FenrirEngine has no way to play sound: no package for music, sound effects or positioned sound, from C++ or C#.

## Who needs it

Flightless Frenzy ([map](../../flightless-frenzy/map.md)). The 2022 game planned background music and effects for jumping, freeing friends and collecting coins.

## Does it block the game?

No. Audio is out of scope for the game's first spec; the game is planned and built silent, and gains sound once this is closed.
