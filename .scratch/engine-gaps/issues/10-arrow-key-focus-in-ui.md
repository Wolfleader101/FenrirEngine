# Arrow keys do not move focus in FenrirUI

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: none

## What is missing

FenrirUI moves focus between controls with Tab and with a gamepad. The keyboard's arrow keys do not move it.

## Who needs it

Flightless Frenzy ([map](../../flightless-frenzy/map.md)): title, pause and results menus that a keyboard player moves through with the arrow keys or W and S, as they would with a gamepad.

## Does anything plan it?

No.

## Does it block the game?

No. Menus work with the mouse, Tab and a gamepad today.
