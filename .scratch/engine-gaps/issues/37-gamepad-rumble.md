# No gamepad rumble

Status: needs-triage
Blocked by: none

## What is missing

FenrirInput reads a gamepad's buttons and axes but cannot make it vibrate. Its README lists rumble under "Not here yet" as out of scope.

## Who needs it

Flightless Frenzy ([map](../../flightless-frenzy/map.md)): a short pulse on a stomp, on a hit from an enemy and on a hard landing ([What does the player play with?](../../flightless-frenzy/issues/07-controls-and-input-devices.md)).

## Does anything plan it?

No.

## Does it block the game?

No. It is wanted for feel on a gamepad; the game is complete without it.
