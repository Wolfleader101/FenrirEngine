# 01: Vehicle tracer bullet: a car that drives, brakes and steers

**What to build:** FenrirPhysics has vehicles end to end, thinly: a default four-wheeled car on a dynamic chassis accelerates through an automatic gearbox, brakes, steers, rides over bumps and reads back its state, in tests and in a DemoApp "Vehicles (C++)" scene on F6 (spec: "FenrirPhysics: the components", "settings and defaults" (the wheeled kind's defaults only), "state", "the step in `PostTick`", "wheel poses and interpolation" (poses only), "the Jolt backend"; ADR-0015, ADR-0010, ADR-0011). The spike on `prototype/fenrir-physics-vehicles-spike` built this slice crudely; build it properly against the spec's API. The spike branch is reference only.

- **Components:** `VehicleInput`, `VehicleProbe`, `Vehicle`, `VehicleTrackSide`, `VehicleWheel`, `VehicleSuspension`, `VehicleTyre`, `VehicleEngine`, `TransmissionMode`, `VehicleTransmission`, `VehicleState` and `VehicleWheelState`, declared with every field and default from the spec and documented (input persists and physics never writes it; `state` is overwritten each write-back with one collision step of lag; `VehicleSuspension::spring`'s damping note; wheel entities kept unscaled and out of the chassis's hierarchy, and the chassis unscaled). `VehicleKind` is a plain-data tagged union in `CollisionShape`'s and `JointKind`'s pattern (`Type()` returning `VehicleType`, asserting `Is<T>()`/`As<T>()`, implicit conversions), defaulting to `WheeledVehicle`, which is its only alternative here; tickets 07–09 add the other three. Fields not wired up here (`probe`, `maxTilt`, `enabled`, the per-wheel angles, `maxHandbrakeTorque`, the Manual gearbox) are declared with their defaults and wired in later tickets. `VehicleDifferential` and `VehicleAntiRollBar` come in ticket 04.
- **`Rpm`** is spelled that way everywhere (`minRpm`, `engineRpm`).
- **`PostTick`:** the `Vehicle` and `VehicleWheel` watches drained with the body, joint and character watches by net effect; backend vehicles removed with the constraints before bodies, created after bodies and joints, in chassis entity order; wheel set changes mark the vehicle for a rebuild (a plain remake here; carrying RPM and spin over is ticket 05). Step 2 diffs vehicles after bodies and characters, beside joint sync, and pushes driver input; any input change wakes the chassis. Step 4 runs each vehicle as the backend's step listener, in parallel across vehicles. Step 5 writes back the chassis, then `state` on the vehicle and its wheels, then wheel poses.
- **The all-wheel-drive default:** a wheeled vehicle drives every wheel equally through an automatic five-speed gearbox, built from the shape fields (the torque curve from the engine's fractions, geometric forward ratios, each tyre's curve from its four numbers). Driver input is clamped silently to its ranges.
- **State:** every `VehicleState` field a wheeled vehicle has (speed, RPM, gear, clutch, shifting, wheels on ground) and every `VehicleWheelState` field (ground body and collider by sub-shape, contact point and normal, ground velocity, compression clamped to `0..travel`, suspension force, slips, spin, steer), empty or zero off the ground. A ground entity destroyed before game code reads it is detected by its version.
- **Wheel poses:** before the first step at `position`; from step 5 on, world position and rotation through `SetWorldTransform`: the chassis pose × `position` raised by `compression`, then steer, then spin about the axle (X). Scale never written; game writes to a wheel `Transform` overwritten. Interpolation is ticket 11; here the wheel takes the chassis's step pose.
- **Backend:** vehicle operations on the backend class (the spike's `CreateVehicle`, `DestroyVehicle`, `UpdateVehicle`, `SetVehicleInput`, `ReadVehicle`, `WheelPose`); a `VehicleConstraint` with the wheeled controller, added with `AddConstraint` and `AddStepListener` and removed (both) before its body; the cylinder collision tester from the chassis's object layer, with a body filter dropping the chassis (characters' inner bodies in ticket 10), reading data fixed before the step and never locking. **The suspension mapping** (spike lesson 1): the hard point a private margin (the wheel's radius) above the top of the travel, min length = margin, max = travel + margin. Each vehicle owns an unshared copy of its wheel settings. Priority from the chassis entity's order; Jolt's vehicle drawing compiled out. Destroying a world removes every vehicle with the constraints, before any body. A paused world steps no vehicle and keeps `state`.
- **ADR-0015 amended** (spec "Further Notes"): four `VehicleKind` alternatives with `TrailerVehicle`; `VehicleTrackSide track`; track ratios in the shared `VehicleInput`; wheel entities out of the chassis's hierarchy; `Vehicle` gains `probe`, `maxTilt` and `state`, `VehicleWheel` its settings and `state`.
- **DemoApp:** "Vehicles (C++)" registers on F6 (`DemoFamilyKey::vehicles`, Default ⇄ Vehicles (C++)) with the spike's tarmac (0.5) start straight, ten bumps and the 2 m ramp, and the 1500 kg car (unscaled chassis with a scaled look child; unscaled wheel entities, not children of the chassis, each with a sphere-and-stripe look child). A C++ chase camera writes `DemoCamera`; C frees it (WASD flies, the arrows still drive) and snaps back. Keys: W/S and arrows throttle (S brakes, then reverses once stopped), A/D steer, Space handbrake, L logs the full state, P autopilot (a lap of the bumps and ramp). The chassis's `Name` and each wheel's show the readouts every frame in the spec's format (km/h converted by the demo).
- **Scene layout for later tickets:** one function per track piece and vehicle, and one autopilot leg per piece, so tickets 02–14 each add their own pieces, keys and legs without editing each other's. Each ticket that adds a piece or key extends the autopilot route and the smoke keys.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] `FenrirPhysicsTests` (through the frame seam): every component's `{}` has the spec's defaults; the default car does 0–100 km/h near the spike's 8.8 s on flat tarmac, shifting up through Automatic, and brakes to a stop; a wheel with no `maxSteerAngle` never steers and one with it steers with `steer`; `input` is never written by physics.
- [ ] State: on a known drive the `VehicleState` fields and each `VehicleWheelState` field read as expected; `compression` stays within `0..travel`; ground fields name the floor's body and collider; in the air they are empty and zero.
- [ ] A bottomed-out wheel stops the chassis (the private margin): a car dropped from height does not sink its chassis into the floor.
- [ ] Wheel poses: at `position` before the first step; with every angle 0 the wheel's rotation equals the chassis's, X the axle; scale never written; a game write to a wheel `Transform` is overwritten.
- [ ] Input wakes a sleeping chassis; a vehicle with no wheels is valid and inert; adding and removing a wheel rebuilds the vehicle.
- [ ] Two runs of the car over the bumps are bit-identical over 600 ticks, single- and multi-threaded; a paused world changes nothing; destroying a world with a vehicle is clean.
- [ ] ADR-0015 carries the five amendments.
- [ ] No public FenrirPhysics header names Jolt; Clang and MSVC x64 debug and release presets build without new warnings.
- [ ] `FENRIR_DEMO_SCENE="Vehicles (C++)"` with `FENRIR_DEMO_KEYS=1` runs the autopilot and keys with no errors, in Debug and Release.
- [ ] F6 toggles Default ⇄ Vehicles (C++); the camera chases, C frees it and snaps back; the car drives over the bumps and ramp with its wheels spinning, steering and compressing; the readouts are live (checked by hand).
