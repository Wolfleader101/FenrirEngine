# 13: Fenrir.Skeleton, Fenrir.Animation and SkinnedMesh give C# the same animation powers as C++, with the C# demo scenes

**What to build:** C# sets up and drives animation as C++ does: the mirrors, per-bone reads and pose copies, base-pose writes, pose edits inside `Modify`, overrides and weights, attachments and detach, playback, seeks, events, clip-event authoring, bone masks and tick-posed timing, with systems joining the `Animate`, `Modify` and `Late` stages, shown in AnimationDemo's scenes 8 and 9 (spec: "Scripting: `Fenrir.Skeleton`, `Fenrir.Animation` and `SkinnedMesh`"; ADR-0039, ADR-0040). Last, because the user chose to build all C# once the C++ animation work is complete (2026-10-29).

- **Packaging:** `Skeletons::ScriptingExtension()` in `FenrirSkeletonScripting` with its contract in `packages/FenrirSkeleton/Managed/Fenrir.Skeleton/`; `Animation::ScriptingExtension()` in `FenrirAnimationScripting` with `packages/FenrirAnimation/Managed/Fenrir.Animation/`; both under `FENRIR_ENABLE_ASSETS` and `FENRIR_ENABLE_SCRIPTING`, laid out and registered like the existing extensions (the shared extension CMake, rules tests, generated natives). The skeleton extension declares `Fenrir.Skeleton.Animate`, `.Modify` and `.Late`, named in C# by `PoseStages`. `SkinnedMesh` is mirrored in `Fenrir.Renderer`, and `Fenrir.Assets`' `AssetHandle<T>` gains the `Skeleton`, `Clip` and `BoneMask` markers.
- **`Fenrir.Skeleton`:** the mirrors (`SkeletonPose` with `Timing`, `BoneAttachment`, `BoneName` with its rejecting `Set`, `Target`) and enums; bones as `int` with −1 for none, `Skeletons.FindBone` and the `SkeletonData` queries by handle (`BoneCount`, `Parent`, `IsDescendant`, names into `Span<char>`, subtrees into `Span<int>`); the per-bone reads with optional `PoseKind` returning `false` when not `Ready` or out of range; the copies `ReadLocals`, `ReadBaseLocals(…, PoseKind)` and `ReadModel` returning the bones written; `PoseState` and `PoseGeneration`; `SetBaseLocal` and `SetBasePose` refused with a log in `Modify` and `Late` and on the other timing during `Animate`; `PoseStages.Timing` and `PoseStages.Step` inside `Animate`, in both the frame and tick passes; `Skeletons.Edit(entity)` in `Modify` returning a `ref struct PoseEdit` with an edit token, a stale token or an `Edit` outside `Modify` writing nothing and logging once; the override functions and weights, `AttachmentState`, `Detach` and the headless `Skeletons.SpawnModel` through the change buffer; no `Ensure`, no palette, no spans over native memory.
- **`Fenrir.Animation`:** `Animator` and `AnimationLayer` with their enums; `Animation.Seek` and `Animation.Playback(entity, out PlaybackInfo)`; `AnimationEvent`, `ClipFinished` and `AnimationEventsDropped` through `EventReader<T>`; `ClipEventId.Of(name)` by the same FNV-1a; `Clips` (`Duration`, `EventCount`, `GetEvent`, names into spans, `FindEvents`, `AddEvent`, `RemoveEvent`); `BoneMasks` (`Create`, `Include`, `Clear`).
- **No-op rules:** the foundation's: `false`, 0, −1 or empty, a debug warning once per call site, nothing thrown, missing packages no-oping.
- **Prefer extending the generators** over hand-written natives where the binding pipeline falls short; bound types only for layouts the generators cannot describe, and the builder names any generator change in the PR.
- **Scenes 8 and 9** in AnimationDemo with README rows and hand checks: *Playback (C#)* with clip control, seeking, playback state and events, plus a crossfade and one masked layer; *Bone attachments (C#)* creating and editing attachments, bone reads and attachment states, with a C# `Modify` system nudging a bone to show stage systems. The demo's scripting is wired as PhysicsDemo's is, built only with `FENRIR_ENABLE_SCRIPTING`.
