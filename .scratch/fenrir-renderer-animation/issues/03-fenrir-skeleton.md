# 03: FenrirSkeleton poses skeletons headlessly, with the pose pipeline, generations, the palette and spawning

**What to build:** the new FenrirSkeleton package: a skeleton entity with a `SkeletonPose` can be posed by hand, read in local, model and world space, driven through the pose pipeline's stages, and spawned from a model, all with no animation, renderer or physics (spec: "Packages, build and install", "FenrirSkeleton: instances and reads", "The pose pipeline and modifiers", "Spawning"; ADR-0028, ADR-0031, ADR-0033, ADR-0035, ADR-0041). Tick-posed timing, the pose override and attachments are later tickets (11, 07, 08), but the types they fill in are declared here where the spec puts them on the component (`PoseTiming`, `PoseKind`).

- **Package:** `packages/FenrirSkeleton/`, built under `FENRIR_ENABLE_ASSETS` with no option of its own, depending on FenrirAssets, FenrirECS, FenrirScene, FenrirApp, FenrirMath and FenrirTime, with its tests and a `PublicHeaders.cpp`. The owner and facade is `Skeletons`. `Skeletons::Install(App&)` registers the skinning source as an app resource and the pipeline's systems, and returns the move-only owner; it fails with one diagnostic and `IsValid() == false` without FenrirAssets; a second registration is logged and ignored; teardown removes the source.
- **Instances:** `SkeletonPose { AssetHandle<Skeleton> skeleton; PoseTiming timing = Frame; }` on the skeleton entity; the private `SkeletonPoses` scene resource holding base locals, final locals, model matrices and the cached palette; Reconcile creating, rebuilding on a handle or revision change, and sweeping; `Ensure(entity)`; `PoseState` (`Ready`, `Unavailable` with empty reads and one log, `Missing`) with recovery rebuilding the instance.
- **Reads and writes:** the spans with their validity rule; `FindBone`, `GetLocal`, `GetModel`, `GetBaseLocal`, `GetBaseModel` (lazy, cached) and `GetBoneWorldMatrix` (`GetWorldMatrix(entity) × M_model[bone]`, `std::optional`); `PoseKind` accepted and, on a frame-posed skeleton, both kinds returning the render pose; `EditBasePose` marking pose-dirty; the three kinds of change kept apart. `PoseGeneration` from one `SkeletonPoses`-wide counter, advanced on finalised-pose change and rebuild, never on a move. `GetPalette` built lazily on the main thread for the current generation and cached with it.
- **Pipeline:** one sequential `PostUpdate` system on the active scene running Reconcile, `Animate`, Prepare, `Modify`, Finalise, Attach (empty until ticket 08) and `Late`; `AddModifier`, `AddAnimate` and `AddLate` with `int` order, ties by registration, handles that remove outside the pipeline, `ModifierOrder::Override = 1000`; inactive scenes paused without catch-up. The `Animate` item context gives the pass's timing, step and instances (frame pass only until ticket 11), and `EditBasePose` on the other timing during `Animate` is refused with one log per call site.
- **Modifiers:** `PoseEdit` with `Base()`, `Local(bone)`, `Model(bone)` and `Write(bone, Target)`; `Target { space, trs, channels, weight }`; each write resolving at once (world through the inverse skeleton world matrix read at `Modify` start, model through the parent's current model matrix), blended in local space for the masked channels, writes composing sequentially, model matrices invalidated and recomputed lazily; a missing inverse skipped with a rate-limited warning; shear dropped; clean instances skipping Prepare and Finalise.
- **Skinning source:** FenrirSkeleton's implementation of the FenrirAssets interface answering `PoseState`, the handle, generation, palette and model matrices per scene and entity.
- **Spawning:** `SpawnSkeletons(scene, model, spawned)` adding `SkeletonPose` to owners whose skeleton loaded and calling `Ensure` (the attachment step of ordinary nodes below bones is ticket 08), and `Skeletons::SpawnModel(scene, model, parent)` for headless apps.
- **Docs:** the package README, GLOSSARY entries for the pose vocabulary, and the root `CMakeLists.txt` adding the package after FenrirAssets.

No demo scene: Seam 8 is the proof. The renderer does not link the package and still draws nothing skinned.
