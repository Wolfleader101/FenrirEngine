# FenrirPainter

The painter (ADR-0060): a value over one `DrawList2D` that records analytic shapes, paths, paints and clips through its own shader, `builtin:painter`, and its own fat vertex. FenrirDraw2D learns nothing about either; the renderer draws the list as it draws any other. The recording depends on FenrirDraw2D (with the FenrirAssets types it carries: asset handles, samplers, image data), FenrirMath and FenrirText, whose paragraphs and glyph runs it draws (so it needs `FENRIR_ENABLE_TEXT`); `Painting::Install`, which registers the shader with the asset store, also needs FenrirApp. It is built with `FENRIR_ENABLE_RENDERER`, whose `fenrir_add_shaders` builds the shader.

## Installing

`Painting::Install(app)` (after FenrirAssets' `Assets::Install`) registers `builtin:painter` with the asset store, as ADR-0027 lets a UI bring its shaders; `GetShader()` is its handle, or load it by name. With no asset store it logs why and the owner's `IsValid()` is false.

## Painting

`FenrirPainter/Painter.hpp`. Make a `Painter` over each frame's list with a `PainterConfig`: the shader, the root scale (logical units per UI unit), the root's blend space (`Gamma` for a UI on the window overlay, `Linear` in an image or the world, ADR-0065), pixel snapping with the target's pixels per unit, the index width, and the stop texture. It keeps nothing between frames.

- **Draws**, each one quad: `DrawRect`, `DrawRoundedRect` (per-corner radii, scaled down together when they do not fit, as CSS does), `DrawBorder` (per-side widths and colours, split at the corners' diagonals), `DrawShadow` (offset, blur, spread, outset or inset), `DrawCircle`, `DrawArc` (a band with flat ends; a whole turn is a ring), `DrawRing`, and `DrawImage` (stretched, or nine-sliced into nine quads whose seams are not anti-aliased).
- **Paths** (`FenrirPainter/Path.hpp`): a `Path` is a value in UI units the caller keeps, copies and transforms (`Transformed`): `MoveTo`, `LineTo`, `QuadTo`, `CubicTo`, `Arc` (centre, radius, start, sweep, clockwise on screen), `ArcTo` (the browser's tangent arc), `Close`, and the helpers `AddRect`, `AddRoundedRect`, `AddCircle`, `AddEllipse`, `AddPolygon` and `AddPolyline`; arcs are kept as cubics. Its fill rule is non-zero unless `SetFillRule(FillRule::EvenOdd)`. `FillPath` flattens it under the current transform to `PainterConfig::tolerance` pixels (a quarter by default), splits the result by winding into loops that cross nowhere (segments split where they meet, each edge kept where its two sides differ under the rule), bridges each hole into the loop round it, and ear-clips them into triangles of shape kind `Triangle`, with a fringe ring a pixel wide about every loop: its inner vertices half a pixel inside the edge, fully covered, its outer ones half a pixel outside, not covered, so the shader applies only the paint, the clip and that coverage. A fill thinner than a pixel is covered too much where its inner vertices cross. Winding is found by rays tested against the edges sorted into bands, and segments are split where they meet on a grid of 1/256 of a logical unit.
- **Strokes**: `StrokePath` with a `StrokeStyle`: width (zero is a hairline, one pixel whatever the transform; a stroke thinner than a pixel is drawn a pixel wide, in a colour faded to its width, since the fringes either side of a thinner one would fold over each other), cap (butt, round, square), join (miter with SVG's limit, round, bevel), and dashes with an offset (a dash of no length is a dot of its round or square caps, as SVG draws it). Each subpath's outline is offset to both sides and joined, the inner side of a join through its corner, so the outline is every segment's rectangle and every join and cap wound one way and its non-zero fill covers each point once. `Path::StrokeOutline` gives that outline as a path, and filling it is the same geometry. A circle, or in a colour a rectangle or rounded rectangle, made by one helper and stroked with no dashes is one analytic `DrawRing` or `DrawBorder` quad instead, unless its sharp corners would not miter.
- **Paint**, per call (`FenrirPainter/Paint.hpp`): a `Colour`, a `LinearGradient` (two points, stops, pad or repeat), a `RadialGradient` (centre, radius, focal point, stops) or an `ImagePattern` (image, sampler, rectangle, tint), each with its own `Transform2D`. Colours are `Colour`s with straight alpha; the vertex holds them sRGB-encoded and premultiplied, and the pipeline's input colour space is sRGB.
- **Stops**: two travel in the vertex. More need a `StopTexture`, the caller's, kept across frames: each frame the painter adds a row per distinct set of stops, and before the list is drawn the caller writes them, `store.Replace(stops.GetImage(), stops.TakeImageData())`. Without one, or with it full, a gradient uses its first and last stops.
- **State**: `Save` and `Restore` push and pop the transform (`Translate`, `Scale`, `Rotate`, `Concat`, applied before the current one) and the clip (`ClipRect`, `ClipRoundedRect`, under the current transform). The command's hardware scissor is the clip stack's intersected bounds; the innermost clip is evaluated per pixel with its radii and rotation, and two axis-aligned sharp rectangle clips are met exactly. A clip the shader cannot express as one rounded rectangle, a path or several rounded clips, is a mask.
- **Text** (ADR-0062): `DrawText(paragraph, point)` draws a FenrirText `Paragraph`'s glyph runs, and `DrawGlyphs(run, point)` one run, as one quad a glyph of shape kind `Glyph` over its atlas rectangle: the run's page in the image slot, its colour the solid paint, under the transform and clip, batched with the shapes (a second face's page starts another command). A paragraph's positions are the target's pixels: lay it out with its sizes and width multiplied by the root scale and the target's pixels per unit, the size its bitmaps are rasterised at, and the painter divides them back so each glyph's whole-pixel rectangle lands on whole pixels from `point`, which pixel snapping moves to a whole pixel. A paragraph with text that was never laid out is laid out on the spot with `PainterConfig::fonts`, to no width limit. `DrawText(paragraph, point, TextShadow{offset, colour})` draws the runs moved and tinted first. A run's decoration (underline, line-through, overline) is a rectangle from its metrics, in its colour.
- **Groups and masks**, recorded as FenrirDraw2D's offscreen groups, which the renderer realises (`packages/FenrirRenderer/README.md`): `BeginGroup(bounds, opacity, blend, filter)` and `EndGroup` draw what is between them into an image of its own over `bounds` (under the current transform, its axis-aligned bounds, clipped to the scissor) and composite it as one at an opacity, by a blend, through a `GroupFilter` (`Blur(radius)`, or `DropShadow(offset, radius, colour)`; lengths in UI units, the colour straight alpha). `BeginMask(path)` fills `path` opaque into a mask group over its bounds and `EndMask` ends its reach: what is drawn between has its colour multiplied by that fill's coverage. `BackdropBlur(rect, radius)` blurs what is already drawn under `rect`, as a frosted panel's background. They nest with each other and apart from `Save` and `Restore`; an end with nothing of its kind innermost appends nothing, and one left open leaves the list unbalanced, which the renderer skips whole.

## The fat vertex and the shader

`PainterVertex` (`FenrirPainter/PainterVertex.hpp`), 140 bytes: the position, the paint's coordinate (`uv`, the paint's transform already applied), the point in the shape's and the clip's own spaces, the shape's and the clip's half sizes and radii, the shape's numbers (border widths, shadow parameters, arc angles, ring radii), the paint's numbers (focal point, stop offsets or stop row), two colours, four border colours, and the shape kind, paint kind, flags and image slot. `PainterVertexLayout()` describes it in fifteen attributes, and `PainterPipeline()` is the painter's pipeline description: `builtin:painter`, `PremultipliedAlpha`, sRGB input, the root's blend space.

`builtin:painter` (`Shaders/painter.vert`, `Shaders/painter.frag`) evaluates each quad's shape as a signed distance in its own space, anti-aliased over one pixel measured by the derivatives, so it stays crisp under any scale or rotation; a glyph's quad samples its atlas page's R8 coverage at `uv`, in the page's texels, and multiplies its paint by it. Its `GAMMA` variant is the one the `Gamma` blend space draws with. Set 1 holds no parameter block, then the image (slot 0) and the stop texture (slot 1); its `MASK` variant, which the renderer draws a command a mask reaches with, adds a parameter block holding `maskRect` and the `mask` texture after them, both the renderer's to fill.

A path's triangles carry the same vertex: the coverage travels in `local.x`, the paint and clip as a shape's do.

**Batching**: consecutive draws, shapes and paths alike, with the same scissor and texture slots extend one command; a texture in a slot the command already filled with another, a scissor change, or an offscreen group's begin or end between them starts another. With 16-bit indices a command past 65535 vertices starts another with a base vertex; `IndexType2D::UInt32` never needs to.

## Tests

With `FENRIR_BUILD_TESTS`: `FenrirPainterTests` checks what each draw appends to a list (groups, masks and backdrop blurs balanced and nested, their bounds and composites in logical units; shape data, premultiplied colours, gradients' stops and transforms, the stop texture's rows, nine-slice quads, the clip stack's scissor and vertex clip, batching and index rollover; paths' triangles and fringes, fill rules, holes and flattening tolerance; strokes' dashes, joins, caps, hairlines and their analytic circles and borders; text's glyph quads over their atlas rectangles, split by page, rotated, clipped, shadowed, decorated, scaled, snapped and laid out on the spot), and, on the renderer's null backend, that `Painting::Install` registers the shader that the painter's pipeline makes one device pipeline, cached by value, with a variant for each blend space, and that a masked run draws through the `MASK` variant with the mask's image and rectangle bound. `FenrirPainterGlTests` (CTest label `gl`, skipped without a display or an OpenGL 4.6 core context) paints a rounded rectangle, a radial gradient, a bordered box, a stroked star, a filled ring with a hole, a half-opacity group, a blurred disc, a masked gradient, a backdrop blur and a word of text at two sizes into an image 2D target (`ImageTargets2D`, ADR-0060) through the renderer on OpenGL and reads back their centres, joins, edges and blurred fall-offs.

RendererDemo's Painter scene (`examples/RendererDemo/README.md`) shows every shape draw on the window overlay; paths and strokes are not in it yet.
